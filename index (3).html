Building the team

Before the work starts

We search, shortlist and negotiate every party the project depends on.

General contractor selection

Prequalification, tender packages and bid levelling. The lowest number is rarely the best contract.

Design & architecture studio

Search, shortlist and tender the studio matched to the concept, the budget and the programme.

Specialist trade partners

Access to the partner the concept depends on — chef, master craftsman or technical specialist — terms negotiated on the owner's side.

Operator & management company

Local and international operators shortlisted and compared against the owner's return, not the brand's.

Specialist consultants

Structural, MEP, lighting and acoustics — the disciplines that decide whether the design works in practice.

Key leadership & staff

The hiring plan for the roles that open the asset: general manager, technical leads and department heads.

Brand, marketing & PR

Agency selection, identity and a pre-opening campaign timed to the licence, not to hope.

Suppliers & vendors

FF&E, technology and operational supplies tendered owner-side against approved vendor lists.

Legal & licensing advisors

The right lawyers and licensing specialists, briefed so approvals never sit on the critical path.

Core services

The oversight that travels across every sector

Whatever the asset class, the owner needs the same nine capabilities under one accountable line.

Lease & property negotiation

Terms and delivery schedules in the owner's favour, turned into an executable brief. Never sign just with a lawyer's perspective.

Program & schedule development

Aggressive schedule preparation and management to close gaps before they slip.

Design oversight

Critical review of schematic, DD and construction documents against brief and standard.

Design coordination

Aligning the design studio, engineers and contractor so drawings issue on schedule and match on site.

Construction field management

On-site oversight to hold work to drawings, quality, cost and schedule throughout the build.

Cost control & financial forensics

Estimation review and control with value engineering, application review and cost-to-complete.

Independent procurement

Every purchase run on the owner's side of the table, independent of contractor or vendor interests.

Permits & authority management

Holding the approvals timeline against the programme, coordinating agencies and inspections.

Pre-opening & handover

Commissioning, snagging and an audit-ready handover — genuinely ready for opening, sale or occupancy.

Governance & impartiality

The guardrails are the product

The rules that make our advice worth having — the same across every asset class, wherever you build in Europe.

Delivered project — owner-side direction

  • OR / PM separation is inviolable

    Never Owner's Representative and Project Manager on the same asset. Advisory and execution stay in separate hands.

  • No commission without written consent

    Referral and procurement commissions require the owner's prior written disclosure and consent — always, without exception.

  • Change orders excluded from any base

    Cost growth is never upside for us. Change orders are carved out of any fee or commission base.

  • Non-exclusive, fully disclosed

    No contractor, supplier or alliance holds a hidden claim on our advice. The owner's decision stays the owner's.
